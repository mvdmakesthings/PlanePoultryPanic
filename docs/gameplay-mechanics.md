# PLANE POULTRY PANIC

## Complete Gameplay Mechanics Guide

### **Core Game Structure**

**Genre**: Top-down action-adventure with survival elements  
**Style**: 16-bit SNES aesthetic (Zombies Ate My Neighbors inspiration)  
**Duration**: 30-minute linear campaign  
**Perspective**: Top-down view with pixel art graphics  
**Audio**: Chiptune soundtrack reminiscent of 80s arcade games

### **Player Character**

**Identity**: Stranded connecting passenger  
**Goal**: Reach the final departure gate and board the last flight to safety  
**Starting Equipment**: Basic carry-on luggage, wallet, expired boarding pass

### **Movement and Controls**

- **8-directional movement** (classic D-pad style)
- **Action button** for interactions, using items
- **Inventory button** to cycle through collected items
- **Run button** for faster movement (limited stamina)

### **Inventory System**

**Capacity**: Limited slots forcing strategic decisions

**Core Items**:

- **Snacks** (Dual-purpose resource)
  - Use on self: Restore health points
  - Throw at chickens: Create temporary distraction
  - Found in: Food court, vending machines, passenger bags
- **Boarding Pass** (Required for final gate access)
  - Location: Must be found in Security Checkpoint area
  - Purpose: Unlock final departure gate
- **Airport Map** (Navigation aid)
  - Shows terminal layout and current location
  - Reveals locations of key items and exits
- **Maintenance Keys** (Access tool)
  - Unlock supply closets and restricted areas
  - Found on maintenance carts and in staff areas
  - Grant access to additional snacks and shortcuts
- **Fire Extinguisher** (Defensive weapon)
  - Temporarily freezes chickens in place
  - Limited uses per extinguisher
  - Found in emergency stations throughout terminal

### **Enemy System: Chickens**

**Base Chicken States**:

1. **Normal Chickens**
- Wander randomly, peck at ground
- Easily distracted by thrown snacks
- Low threat level
- Can be avoided with careful movement
1. **Powered-Up Chickens** (After drug box consumption)
- **Flight Mode**: Brief aerial movement, harder to avoid
- **Rage Mode**: Increased speed and aggression, red coloration
- **Egg Shooter Mode**: Ranged projectile attacks
- Effects last 30-60 seconds before wearing off

**Chicken Behaviors**:

- **Patrol Patterns**: Some chickens follow predictable routes
- **Alertness**: Chickens can spot players and call for reinforcements
- **Drug Seeking**: Chickens prioritize accessing drug disposal boxes
- **Pack Coordination**: Groups work together to corner players

**Boss Mechanic: Chicken Totems**

- Multiple chickens stack vertically to form towering boss entities
- **Formation Triggers**: Large groups of chickens in one area
- **Totem Abilities**:
  - Sustained flight instead of brief bursts
  - Area-of-effect rage attacks
  - Rapid-fire egg bombardment
  - Command nearby chickens
- **Defeat Methods**:
  - Massive snack distractions to break formation
  - Fire extinguisher to freeze base chickens
  - Environmental hazards (baggage carousels, moving walkways)

### **Health and Survival**

**Health System**: Traditional hit points (displayed as luggage icons)

- Start with 3 health points
- Lose 1 point from chicken attacks or egg hits
- Restore health by consuming snacks (1 snack = 1 health point)

**Failure States**:

- Health reaches zero: Restart from last checkpoint
- Time limit: Final flight departure countdown (optional pressure)

**Difficulty Scaling**:

- Chicken density increases in later areas
- More powered-up chickens in advanced zones
- Environmental hazards become more complex

### **Level Progression**

**Linear Path Structure** (30-minute total):

1. **Arrivals/Baggage Claim** (5 minutes)
- Tutorial area introducing basic mechanics
- Simple chicken encounters
- Learn movement, interaction, snack throwing
- Find first maintenance key
1. **Security Checkpoint** (5 minutes)
- Locate boarding pass (hidden in security bins)
- Navigate TSA machinery obstacles
- First powered-up chicken encounters
- Introduction to fire extinguisher
1. **Food Court** (5 minutes)
- Major snack resupply area
- Multiple vending machines and restaurant supplies
- Higher chicken concentration due to food
- First chicken totem mini-boss encounter
1. **Gate Areas** (10 minutes)
- Multiple connected sections (Gates A, B, C)
- Escalating difficulty and chicken varieties
- Navigate around drug disposal boxes
- Complex environmental puzzles
- Regular chicken totem formations
1. **Final Departure Gate** (5 minutes)
- Ultimate chicken totem boss fight
- Requires boarding pass for entry
- Use all learned skills and remaining inventory
- Escape sequence to board final flight

### **Environmental Interactions**

**Airport Infrastructure**:

- **Baggage Carousels**: Can trap or redirect chickens
- **Moving Walkways**: Affect movement speed and chicken behavior
- **Security Scanners**: Create barriers requiring navigation
- **Flight Information Displays**: Provide story context and hints
- **PA System**: Occasional announcements add atmosphere

**Interactive Elements**:

- **Vending Machines**: Snack sources (require coins or maintenance keys)
- **Drug Disposal Boxes**: Chicken power-up stations to avoid or manipulate
- **Emergency Exits**: Potential shortcuts (some may be blocked)
- **Bathroom Stalls**: Hiding spots for temporary safety
- **Gate Seating**: Cover from chicken projectiles

### **Strategic Elements**

**Resource Management**:

- Balance snack consumption vs. distraction use
- Manage fire extinguisher charges carefully
- Plan inventory space for essential items

**Stealth vs. Action**:

- Avoid chicken patrols when possible
- Use distractions to create safe paths
- Direct confrontation as last resort

**Timing Challenges**:

- Wait for chicken power-ups to wear off
- Navigate around drug box feeding times
- Coordinate movements with environmental hazards

### **Victory Conditions**

**Primary Objective**: Board the final flight
**Requirements**:

- Possess valid boarding pass
- Reach final departure gate alive
- Defeat or bypass the ultimate chicken totem boss

**Potential Endings**:

- **Standard Victory**: Successful escape on final flight
- **Perfect Run**: Complete with full health and unused items
- **Speedrun**: Complete under target time
- **Pacifist**: Complete without defeating any chickens (distraction only)

### **Replay Value**

- **High Score System**: Points for survival, efficiency, items collected
- **Achievement System**: Special challenges and objectives
- **Difficulty Modes**: More chickens, less health, fewer resources
- **Speedrun Mode**: Timer challenges for competitive play
- **Easter Eggs**: Hidden references and secret areas
