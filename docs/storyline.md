# PLANE POULTRY PANIC

## Complete Storyline Summary

### **The Awakening**

In a world not unlike our own, chickens lived their ordinary lives on farms, in coops, and in industrial facilities. For generations, they watched enviously as planes soared overhead, carrying humans to distant destinations with an ease that their own flightless wings could never achieve. This silent jealousy festered in their collective consciousness, growing stronger with each passing aircraft.

The catalyst came when a flock of free-range chickens discovered a patch of wild mushrooms growing near a rural airport. These weren’t ordinary mushrooms, but magical psychoactive fungi that had absorbed decades of aviation fuel runoff and electromagnetic radiation from overhead flights. As the chickens pecked at these glowing specimens, something extraordinary happened.

### **The Transformation**

The mystical mushrooms triggered a mass awakening. Suddenly, chickens possessed not only sentience and intelligence, but supernatural abilities that made a mockery of their earthbound nature. They could fly, albeit briefly. They could channel their rage into devastating attacks. Most importantly, they could communicate telepathically, forming a global network of grievance against humanity’s monopoly on flight.

But the magic came with a price. The effects were temporary, requiring regular “refueling” to maintain their powers. The chickens quickly discovered that human drug disposal boxes, particularly those found in airports, contained pharmaceutical compounds that could extend and amplify their abilities.

### **The Fall of Civilization**

What followed was swift and devastating. Coordinated chicken attacks targeted airports worldwide. Runways were blocked by massive flocks. Air traffic control towers were overtaken by telepathically-linked chicken swarms. Commercial aviation ground to a halt as chickens systematically dismantled humanity’s greatest achievement.

Without air travel, global supply chains collapsed. International communication broke down. Governments fell as they couldn’t coordinate responses across continents. In a matter of weeks, the world’s infrastructure crumbled under the weight of chicken-orchestrated chaos.

Airports, once symbols of human ingenuity and connection, became monuments to hubris. The chickens claimed them as their territories, establishing hierarchies and power structures within the very spaces that had once mocked their flightlessness.

### **Your Story Begins**

You are a connecting passenger caught in the chaos of the initial chicken uprising. Your original flight was canceled when the terminal was overrun, and you’ve been stranded for days as the outside world descended into apocalyptic chaos.

Now you’ve learned of one final departure, a last flight to safety before the airport is permanently sealed. This isn’t the destination you planned, but it’s your only hope of escape from this feathered nightmare.

### **The Current State**

The airport terminal has become a bizarre ecosystem. Chickens patrol the corridors in organized formations, their supernatural abilities making them formidable guardians. They’ve established feeding stations at drug disposal boxes, creating a hierarchy based on access to pharmaceutical power-ups.

Different areas of the terminal have different chicken populations:

- **Arrivals/Baggage**: Newly awakened chickens, still learning their powers
- **Security**: Elite chickens who’ve mastered the art of confiscation and control
- **Food Court**: Chickens drunk on power and snack access
- **Gates**: Battle-hardened veterans guarding the last symbols of human flight
- **Final Departure Gate**: The ultimate boss, a supreme chicken entity

### **The World Beyond**

Outside the terminal, society has adapted to this new reality. Some humans have learned to coexist with the chicken overlords. Others have formed resistance movements. But within this airport, you face a microcosm of the larger struggle, a personal battle for freedom in a world turned upside down by the most unlikely of apocalypses.

### **Themes**

**Hubris and Humility**: The irony of humanity’s greatest achievement in flight being brought down by flightless birds

**Environmental Consequences**: The magical mushrooms represent how human activity (fuel runoff, electromagnetic pollution) can have unexpected consequences

**Perspective**: The story from the chickens’ point of view reveals how human superiority isn’t as natural or permanent as we assume

**Survival and Adaptation**: Your character must navigate a world where the rules have fundamentally changed

**Absurdist Horror**: The blend of genuinely apocalyptic consequences with utterly ridiculous causes creates a unique tone that’s both funny and unsettling

### **How the Story Is Told**

The game is about 30 minutes long and should never stop for long cutscenes. The story comes through:

- **Opening**: A short scene of the stranded passenger at their cancelled gate, holding an expired boarding pass, hearing the PA announce the last flight out.
- **PA announcements and flight displays**: The terminal still runs on autopilot. Announcements and departure boards get stranger as the chickens take over, delivering jokes, hints, and backstory.
- **The environment**: Nests in luggage, pecked-up signs, feeding crowds at drug disposal boxes, and chicken graffiti show how far the takeover has gone in each area.
- **Ending**: The passenger boards with their new boarding pass and the plane takes off. One last shot hints that a chicken may have stowed away.

### **Tone Guardrails**

- Chickens are menacing in a cartoon way, never gory. Defeated chickens vanish in a puff of feathers.
- The jokes come from the absurd situation, not from mocking the player.
- The drug disposal boxes stay cartoonish: glowing, rattling boxes that power chickens up, with no realistic drug use shown.
