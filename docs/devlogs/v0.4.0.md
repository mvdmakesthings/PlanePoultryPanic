# New look, same layover (v0.4.0)

itch.io devlog for v0.4.0. Cover: `docs/images/devlog_v0.4.0.png`.

---

Amanda has a new look.

Until now she was a first-pass sprite drawn by code: a pink helmet of hair, two dots for eyes and a rectangle for a shirt. This update replaces her with sprites made straight from her reference art, so she finally looks like the person on the concept sheet: shoulder-length mauve-pink hair with side-swept bangs, a gray tee with a little crest on it, the blue backpack, black leggings, chunky charcoal boots, and the tattoos on her forearms.

## What's new

- **A real sprint.** Her run is now a full stride: long steps, arms pumping, hair streaming out behind her. It's drawn in bigger frames because the old ones couldn't fit her legs.
- **Four-way walking** with a proper front, back and both sides, and an idle that breathes.
- **Everything else follows.** Throwing, flinching, eating, getting knocked out, the extinguisher in her hand and the hair that gets messier as she takes hits were all rebuilt on the new sprites.

Nothing about how the game plays has changed: same levels, same chickens, same layover.

One thing to know: running straight toward or away from the camera uses a three-quarter view for now, so she looks like she's sprinting sideways for a moment when you head up or down the screen. A head-on run is on the list.

The sheets were converted from the reference art by a small tool, which also means that the next time her look changes, the game can pick it up the same day.

Play it in your browser at https://planepoultrypanic.mvdmakesthings.com/ or right here on itch.io.
